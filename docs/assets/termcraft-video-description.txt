termcraft | Terminal based multiplayer 2D survival

A whole survival world. Inside your terminal.

Gather resources, build a home, explore villages and caves, smelt iron, craft armour and mine diamonds. Then head into the Nether and face the Ender Dragon—with your friends.

Termcraft is a free, open-source, Minecraft-inspired 2D survival game for Linux, macOS and Windows. Terminal pixel art, multiplayer for up to eight players, and three dimensions to explore.

This showcase features actual multiplayer gameplay in the detailed terminal view.
