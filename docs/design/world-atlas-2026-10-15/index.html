Coverage and resolved findings

This tour covers all ten current Overworld biomes, the three dimensions, village building roles, both dungeon types, the stronghold rooms, Nether fortress sections, the route-landmark family, End towers/portals/islands and representative survival, creature, weather and UI scenarios.

Nether route variants: the marker-seed bug is fixed. Shrines, bridges and reliquaries now spawn naturally; all three cards show discovered native structures. Entrances, walking cutaways, supported loot and roof-mounted lights keep these landmarks usable in the side view.

The selected front-facing dragon is now the playable default, with matching combat regions. Its overview and detail cards show the same adopted artwork. The deep lava-layer scene is a cutaway inspection, rather than a safe spawn. All images come from the renderer; the scene labels identify staged positions, light and UI.
